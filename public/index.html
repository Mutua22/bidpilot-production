FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything important before you choose a product.

What is BidPilot?

BidPilot is the product brand for focused bidding workflow tools. You can choose the StudyPool Bidding Bot or EssayPro Bidding Bot from the Products section.

Can I use one license on multiple devices?

No. A license is bound to one device at a time.

Which plans are available?

Both products offer 7-Day, Monthly, Quarterly and Annual access.

How do I receive my license?

After a successful M-Pesa payment, the BidPilot system creates your license and shows it in the checkout status.

Where do I download my product?

Use the Download Center. StudyPool has Windows and macOS packages, while EssayPro has its Chrome extension package.

Are card and PayPal payments available?

M-Pesa is the currently enabled payment method. Card and PayPal can be enabled later when configured.
